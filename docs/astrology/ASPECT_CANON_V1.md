## 0. Origin rule (locked)

Same tags as Planet / Sign / House Canon fill.

| Origin | Means | Does not mean |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **direct** | Normalization of a 1.3.94 **include** family (same concept, shorter lemma) | Copied vendor sentence |
| **direct-secondary** | Normalization of a 1.3.94 **secondary** family | A second stem that displaces include |
| **derived** | TodayFlow inference from **two or more** locked families of **this** aspect | A new astrology; a school package; Google |

`derived` is not weaker. It marks where our model starts. This fill uses **direct** only. No derived atom was required.

**Reject** if: not in that aspect’s 1.3.94 families even as parents; is an outcome (growth / success / failure); is a moral; stamps valence good/bad; copies a planet `core_function` / constructive / distorted; equates opposition with House 7; uses hard/soft or element arithmetic as proof; is pair-specific; or is user-facing copy.

Pipeline per atom:

```text
lemma → direct | direct-secondary | derived → parent family/families → five gates → keep / rewrite / drop
```

## 1. Five gates (locked)

1. **Origin control** — every `relation` atom is a 1.3.94 include or secondary on that aspect, or derived only from those families. No classification arithmetic. No fourth panel. No books.
2. **Relation-fit** — the lemma must bind **several** planet pairs, not only one convenient pair. Square must work on Mars×Saturn and Venus×Saturn **without planet-keyed aspect lemmas**.
3. **Collision control** — the lemma is topology of a link, not a planet verb, not a house arena, not an outcome ([grammar §2.4–§2.5](./ASPECT_CANON_GRAMMAR_V1.md)).
4. **Discrimination** — the five operators must be distinguishable. Locked pairs: **square ↔ opposition** · **trine ↔ sextile** · **conjunction ↔ trine**. Also keep square ↔ trine.
5. **Topology, not meaning** — no growth / success / failure, no “challenge makes you stronger,” no good/bad stamp. Conjunction pack stays **mixed-valence** as a guard: blend/fuse only; quality waits for the two functions.

Coverage is **not** “every 1.3.94 family placed.” Expected: **3** relation atoms per aspect. Leftover families stay recognizable as territory. A lemma that looks too technical for a Today line and still composes is **kept**.
