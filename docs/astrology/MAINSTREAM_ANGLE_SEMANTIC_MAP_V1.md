## 2. How “mainstream” is judged (no bureaucracy)

**Wrong:** require each lemma to appear as the same string in 2/3 sources.

**Right:** sources sketch a **territory**. Near-synonyms collapse to one **concept family**.

Examples of one family, not three lemmas:

- doorway · solar doorway · enter the world · rising edge → **doorway-meeting**
- first impression (personal) · how presented · how perceived · how-met → **how-met / personal-first-contact**
- public face · image projected to society · mark in the outer world → **public-facing / outer-mark**
- highest point · most public point · culmination → **height / culmination**

A family is **include** when the panel independently points at that **edge** (wording may differ).  
A family is **secondary** when it is present but not the first modern association, or it is a mass shorthand that **collides** with House 1 / House 10 vocabulary.  
A family is **exclude** when it is house-as-proof, angular strength, a planet-on-angle recipe, a sign-on-angle portrait, one school, identity dump (ASC=Aries, MC=Capricorn), DSC/IC as gold objects, or Foundation geometry copied as meaning.

LLM (build-time, supplied field only) may propose family collapse. It does not add a fourth source, vote Google, or lock.
