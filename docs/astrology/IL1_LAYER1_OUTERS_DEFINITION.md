## 4. Definitions (working)

- **Structural constituent (outer):** a lemma that identifies the body as a lookup primitive **without** saying how a person tends to be under it. Coverage ≠ “Hand wrote a function sentence.”
- **Later-interpretive constituent (outer):** a lemma about what the body *does* in a life or psyche (`function` / themes / expression / shadow / natal domains). Same CORE rule as planets: same lemma across **school classes**, not three paraphrases of transformation.
- **Not the same `function` as Sun–Saturn.** Classical seven: elemental quality. Outers: school meaning. Do not silent-collapse those jobs.
- **Not a constituent:** Foundation identity; Swiss coordinates; Layer 5 combinations; product voice.
