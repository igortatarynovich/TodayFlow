**Coverage holes that actually need literature** (not more classical classification):

1. Traditional **classification ontology** (the collision already logged: triplicity ≠ Aristotelian element-on-sign).
2. Psychological **later-interpretive principle** (how motivation/excess/deficiency are *generated* from sign structure — one textbook, not three portrait books).

4. Professional **principle chapter** on signs as a symbol system (not Hand Ch.11 twelve core-meaning portraits first).

Classical classification is **saturated enough to stop hunting**. Further classical volumes are redundancy unless they introduce a *named system not already logged*.
