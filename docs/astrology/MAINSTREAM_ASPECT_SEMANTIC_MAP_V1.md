### 0.2 Aspect is a relation, not a theme (locked guard)

Planet = what. Sign = how. House = where. Aspect = **how two functions meet**.

An aspect must not grow its own topic. If it does, square stops being an operator and becomes a ready-made narrative: “conflict causes growth.” Trine becomes “luck / talent.” Conjunction becomes “new beginnings.” Those are themes. They steal the planets’ jobs.

Forbidden this pass:

```text
square        →  life-lesson / character-building as the stem
trine         →  luck / blessing as a topic
conjunction   →  new-chapter / birth as a topic
opposition    →  partnership-as-7th-house
sextile       →  Jupiter-opportunity as a topic
```

If a family is include, it is because the panel independently points at **that aspect’s way of relating**. Square may include demand-for-action because Cafe names tension that stimulates action **on the square**. Trine must not receive “action” because it is also a major aspect.

### 0.3 Classification is not proof

Existing labels — hard / soft, harmonious / disharmonious, Ptolemy same-sex, element agreement (trine = same element, square = incompatible) — may **later explain** a family. They must not **generate** one.

Forbidden this pass:

```text
hard          →  friction
soft          →  flow
same element  →  trine meaning
incompatible  →  square meaning
Foundation §2.4 «трение, необходимость усилия» as panel evidence
IL Layer 4 slot table (merging / friction requiring action / …) as Mainstream
object.interaction enum (merging / friction / flow / polarization) as proof
```

Cafe’s chart-wide “predominant aspects” portraits (crisis-oriented people, unchallenged people) are **not** evidence for the square or the trine as operators. Use only what those aspect sections say about **that** aspect as a relation.

## 2. How “mainstream” is judged (no bureaucracy)

**Wrong:** require each lemma to appear as the same string in 2/3 sources.

**Right:** sources sketch a **territory**. Near-synonyms collapse to one **concept family**.

Examples of one family, not three lemmas:

- blend · unite · combined energies · act together → **blend / unite**
- blocked · at cross purposes · mutually exclusive directions → **cross-purposes / blockage**
- easy flow · complementary · enriching → **easy-flow / complementary**

A family is **include** when the panel independently points at that territory (wording may differ).  
A family is **secondary** when it is present but not the first modern association, or it is a mass shorthand of the include family.  
A family is **exclude** when it is one school, one author, an orb/applying rule, a dignity rule, a minor, an aspect pattern, a pair recipe, hard/soft arithmetic, Foundation §2.4 copied as proof, or a **theme** pretending to be a relation.

LLM (build-time, supplied field only) may propose family collapse. It does not add a fourth source, vote Google, or lock.

## 4. Concept families (locked)

Panel notes are paraphrases, not copy. Compact Astrodienst line is the reference paragraph. Cafe’s major-aspect body is the mass operator text. Astrology.com shorthand is the contemporary label.

### Conjunction

| | Families |
|--|----------|
| **Include** | blend / unite · fuse / combine · immediate-connection · mixed-valence (quality depends on the two functions) |
| **Secondary** | hard-to-separate / subjective (Cafe) · strengthening (Astrology.com) |
| **Exclude** | conjunction = always harmonious · combustion / “burnt Mercury” · new-beginnings as a life theme · stellium essays · pair recipes (Sun conjunct Mercury) · orbs |

Astrodienst compact: immediate connection; quality depends greatly on the planets involved; tends harmonious but is not guaranteed. Cafe: energies united, blended, act together; closer = more subjective; difficult to acknowledge as distinct. Astrology.com: combined energies strengthen for better or worse; beginners list: fusion due to close proximity.

`mixed-valence` is include because the panel refuses a single good/bad stamp **on the conjunction**, not because Foundation says “нейтральный.” Combustion is a pair-condition. Out.

### Opposition

| | Families |
|--|----------|
| **Include** | polarity / facing · tension-across · the-other / mirror-through-another · awareness-of-opposite |
| **Secondary** | swing / waver (Cafe) · motivating / energizing (Astrodienst) · consider-the-other-side (Cafe) |
| **Exclude** | opposition = 7th house · opposition = square · projection-as-the-only-stem · “learning experience” as product copy · T-square · pair recipes · orbs |

Astrodienst compact: generally disharmonious or dynamic; creates tension, often with positive results; quality depends on the planets and on what one makes of it. Cafe: “it takes two”; relationship; learn about themselves through others; swing from one side to the other; torn; vs square: opposition is unsure/wavering, square is urgent. Astrology.com: opposites on the wheel; bring out best and worst; beginners: awareness of opposing energy.

Opposition **the-other** is a relating mode, not House 7’s arena. Do not collapse them.

### Square

| | Families |
|--|----------|
| **Include** | friction / tension · blockage · cross-purposes · demand-for-action |
| **Secondary** | recurring-obstacle (Astrodienst “bad penny”) · stress-when-overdone (Cafe) |
| **Exclude** | square = growth-narrative / “challenge causes growth” as the stem · square = conflict-as-a-topic · square = Mars · T-square · pair recipes · Foundation §2.4 as proof · IL “friction requiring action” copied as Mainstream |

Astrodienst compact: disharmonious; planets seem blocked; problems keep turning up; two forces moving in completely different directions; mutually exclusive desires and needs. Cafe: tension between the points; tension stimulates action; too much = stress; acting at cross purposes; obstacles before “getting it right”; force out of complacency. Astrology.com symbols: unfriendly; drama and intense conflict. Beginners: tension; calls for creative resolution.

Cafe “force us to grow and learn our lessons” and Astrology.com “every challenge helps us grow” are **theme-capture**. They stay out of include. The operator is how the two functions meet (friction / blockage / cross-purpose / action-demand), not a ready-made character arc.

`demand-for-action` is include because Cafe and the beginners page name action/resolution **on the square**, not because the schema field is `requires_action`. That boolean stays unevidenced. Do not promote it here.

### Trine

| | Families |
|--|----------|
| **Include** | easy-flow · support / complementary · natural-ease · talents-unconscious |
| **Secondary** | accepting (Cafe) · luck (Astrology.com mass shorthand of ease) · unused-unless-chosen (Astrodienst) |
| **Exclude** | trine = luck as destiny · trine = Jupiter · grand trine · same-element arithmetic as proof · pair recipes (Venus trine Neptune) · “good vibes” as Canon |

Astrodienst compact: harmonious; work together in a complementary fashion, enriching one another; show where natural talents lie, whether used or not. Cafe: support; accepting; talents so natural they are almost unconscious; may not develop them; second nature. Astrology.com: lucky, harmony, positive changes, good vibes; beginners: natural flow/exchange of energy.

`luck` is secondary mass of ease, same caution as planet Jupiter: convention, not unstructured function. Trine does **not** inherit conjunction’s merge. The two functions remain distinct and cooperate.

### Sextile

| | Families |
|--|----------|
| **Include** | ease-with-participation · opportunity · cooperation · directed-potential |
| **Secondary** | more-overt-than-trine (Cafe) · friendly / chill (Astrology.com) · communicative (Cafe) |
| **Exclude** | sextile = trine · sextile = Jupiter-opportunity as a topic · minor-as-major · element-pair arithmetic (fire-air / earth-water) as proof · pair recipes · IL “opportunity requiring participation” copied as Mainstream |

Astrodienst compact: tends to have a harmonious effect, depending on the planets involved (thin on purpose — do not pad from trine). Cafe: often read like a trine, but distinct; talent and ease with more “oomph”; more overt; notice and work on these talents; energies capable of being directed; intelligent use; remain separate and appreciate cooperation; conscious effort to push into constructive channels. Astrology.com: chill, friendly, get along; beginners: an opportunity for manifestation.

Sextile **opportunity** is a relating mode (ease available to be used). It is not Jupiter’s `expand` / `opportunity` as a planet theme. Keep the jobs apart.
