DsCard is the base surface for any grouped content — eight variants cover warm-cream standard cards through glass overlays to the dark "close of day" surface.

```jsx
<DsCard variant="insight">
  <DsStatusBadge>Today</DsStatusBadge>
  <p>Your theme: one main thread, not five urgent ones.</p>
</DsCard>
```

`variant="feature"` renders a 6px gold accent rail on the left edge. `size="compact"` switches to the tighter Surface-B padding used inside Today blocks. `DsStatusBadge` is the small uppercase pill used for section/status labels inside a card.
